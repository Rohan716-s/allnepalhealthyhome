## Classification and implementation implications

**Verified legal-source findings:** the IRD rules contain prescribed invoice provisions and conditional abbreviated invoicing; current applicability still requires the latest amendments. DDA publishes a 2080 distribution code. Gazette listings confirm newer tax amendments. These findings are not an entity-specific compliance opinion.

**Standard accounting principles:** supplier settlement debits the liability and credits money; customer settlement debits money and credits the receivable. Each entry must balance, but a balanced entry can still use incorrect accounts. Recognize inventory/COGS using a documented policy and reconcile subledgers to control accounts. These are accounting design rules, not newly discovered Nepal tax provisions.

**Business best practices:** enforce batch/expiry checks, segregate approval duties, maintain recoverable backups, and test replay/concurrency. Configure company registration and product classifications before enabling tax postings.

**Future-compatible recommendations:** effective-dated rules with source/version/approval metadata; immutable transaction rate/treatment snapshots; explicit fiscal period records; supported-range BS conversion; controlled changes and reversals. 3000 BS is an extensibility horizon. No future laws, tax rates or calendar conversion data are asserted.
